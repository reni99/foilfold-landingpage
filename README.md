# FoilFold website

The landing page and privacy policy for FoilFold, a Magic: The Gathering collection app for
iPhone, iPad and Mac. Plain HTML and CSS, served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch.

- `index.html`: the landing page (with a small search demo in plain JavaScript)
- `privacy.html`: the privacy policy (the URL App Store Connect asks for)
- `assets/`: the app icon and App Store screenshots (taken with fastlane in the app's repository)

Card data and images are provided by Scryfall. Magic: The Gathering is © Wizards of the Coast;
FoilFold is not produced or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast or Scryfall.
