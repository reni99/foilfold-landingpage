The Exact Printing

Legendary Enchantment — Printing

Every set, showcase and borderless frame, and every promo. Record the finish, condition and language of each copy.

“Every Swamp has a story. FoilFold knows all 841.”

Binder Page

Artifact — Binder

Pick a set and go through it page by page in binder order. Tap the pockets you own; each shows how many copies you already have.

“Nine pockets at a time, until the set is complete.”

Appraisal

Sorcery — Value

Estimate the value of every copy and of the whole collection, from Cardmarket and TCGplayer prices via Scryfall.

“The rarest thing in a binder is knowing what it’s worth.”

Sort and Filter

Instant — Order

Combine color, mana value, type, rarity and set. Sort in up to three levels, with your own cards first or the gaps first.

“Order from chaos, three levels deep.”

Offline Catalog

Land — Catalog

The whole catalog lives on your device, so search never waits for the network. New sets arrive in the background.

“The basement has no signal. The catalog doesn’t need any.”

Enchantment — Sync

No account to create. Your collection syncs through your own iCloud between iPhone, iPad and Mac. CSV in and out whenever you like.

“What’s in your binder stays in your binder.”
